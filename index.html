Build & Download

      

        Takes the bundled XrAudioSystem.rbxm template, renames the show folder,
        writes every uploaded track from the Process tab into the show's Sound slots (up to
        the template's slot count tracks — read directly from the file, not
        hard-coded), bakes in the whitelist above, and gives you back one updated
        .rbxm — insert it into Studio same as before, no manual editing.
      

      
      
Upload at least one track above first — this builds from whatever's actually been uploaded.

      

    


    

      
Audio Permissions

      

        This one's separate from everything above — it doesn't touch the show file at all. It grants a
        Roblox account permission to actually use your uploaded audio in their own games, via
        Roblox's real asset-permission system (different from the Show-Control Whitelist earlier, which
        only controls in-game show triggering). The typical case: you uploaded from an alt account, and
        your main account (or someone else's) needs to be able to insert these audio IDs into their own
        place. Whoever's connected/API key is used here needs to own the assets being shared.
